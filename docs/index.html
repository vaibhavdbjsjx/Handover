Questions

Good to know

Will a report from Handover be accepted by a deposit scheme?

Deposit schemes and tribunals accept landlord-prepared reports. What matters is the quality of the evidence: detailed descriptions, dated photographs and the tenant's signature. Handover is built around exactly that. It isn't legal advice, and an adjudicator decides each case on its facts.

Does the AI decide my deductions?

No. AI drafts descriptions and suggests whether a change looks like fair wear and tear, cleaning or damage. You review every line and set every amount yourself.

Where are my reports stored?

On your iPhone, included in your normal iCloud or computer backup. Handover has no account and no copy of your reports.

What happens to photos sent to AI?

Only when you tap the AI button, the photos of that room are sent through Handover's server to Google's Gemini service to write the descriptions. They are not stored by Handover and are not used to train AI models.

Can tenants use Handover?

Yes. Tenants use it to record their own move-in condition and protect their deposit.
