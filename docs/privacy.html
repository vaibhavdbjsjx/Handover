Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version
  • Your properties, reports, photos and signatures are stored only on your iPhone. We have no account system and no copy of them.
  • Only when you tap an AI button, the photos of that room are sent through our server to Google's Gemini service to write descriptions. We don't store them, and they aren't used to train AI models.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Handover ("we", "us") is an iPhone app operated by [YOUR LEGAL NAME OR COMPANY], [YOUR POSTAL ADDRESS]. For anything about privacy, email support@handoverapp.co.

Information that stays on your device

Everything you create in Handover is stored locally on your iPhone: property details, tenant and landlord names, reports, photos, meter readings, signatures, compliance dates and your profile details. This data is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) under Apple's terms. We cannot see or access it. When you share a report, you choose who receives the PDF.

If you allow it, photos are tagged with your location when taken. Location is saved only inside your reports on your device and is never sent to us.

AI descriptions and comparisons

AI features run only when you tap "Describe room" or "Compare room", and only after you agree to the in-app explanation. When you do, the app sends the following to our server:

DataWhy
Photos of that room (and, for comparisons, the matching check-in photos), resizedSo the AI can describe or compare what is visible
Room name, room type, item names, check-in descriptions, country and currencySo the result uses the right items, wording and currency
A random identifier created by the app, and the app versionTo apply fair-use daily limits and prevent abuse

Names, addresses, email addresses, signatures and location are never sent. Our server (hosted by Cloudflare) passes the request to the Google Gemini API, receives the result and returns it to your phone. We do not store the photos or the result. Google processes the request as our service provider under the Gemini API terms for paid services, which do not allow your content to be used to improve Google's products. Google may keep requests for a limited period to detect abuse, as described in its terms.

The random identifier is kept in a counter for up to 36 hours to apply daily limits. It isn't linked to your identity.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Evidence & privacy, and keep using Handover without AI.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription.

Notifications

Compliance reminders are scheduled on your device. No reminder data is sent to us.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete the conversation within 24 months.

Legal bases (UK and EU)

We process AI requests on the basis of your consent and to provide the service you asked for. We apply rate limits on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service available and secure. Our service providers may process data in the United States and elsewhere under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Because we don't hold your reports or any account, the data is under your control on your device. You can delete any property or report in the app, or everything at once in Settings > Delete all data. Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, Australian Privacy Act or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Contact us and we'll help. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Tenants and other people in reports

Landlords who use Handover decide what goes in their reports and are responsible for handling tenants' information lawfully. Tenants who have questions about a report should contact the landlord or agent who created it.

Children

Handover is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
