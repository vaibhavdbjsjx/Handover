Getting started

Create a property. Tap + on the Properties tab. Handover builds the room list from the bedrooms and bathrooms you enter.

Start a check-in. Open the property and tap "New check-in report". Open each room, take photos, then tap "Describe room" to let AI fill in every item.

Sign and finalise. Add signatures for the tenant and landlord, then tap Finalise to lock the report. Tap "View & share report" to export the PDF.

At the end of the tenancy. Tap "Check-out". Each room is copied from the check-in. Retake photos using the overlay to line them up, then tap "Compare room".

Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in Handover and tap "Restore purchases". Your reports move to the new iPhone when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup.

The AI button says I've reached a limit

Free accounts include three AI rooms. Pro includes unlimited AI within a generous daily fair-use limit, which resets each day.

Why is my report watermarked?

Previews are watermarked until the report is unlocked. Your first export is free; after that, subscribe to Pro or buy a single report.

Can I edit a signed report?

Signed reports are locked so they stay exactly as signed. You can reopen a report, which removes the signatures so everyone signs the new version.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Handover. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a property or report from its menu, or remove everything in Handover's Settings > Delete all data. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your iPhone.
